Week 4 Report: Advanced Model Evaluation and Hyperparameter Tuning

Summary:
The final week delivered rigorous evaluation and optimization. Implementing a 5-fold cross-validation demonstrated stable model generalization with a mean score of 80.77%. The classification report highlighted strong performance, particularly with 93% recall on non-survivors, validating the system as highly reliable.
